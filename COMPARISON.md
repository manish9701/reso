# Comparison — which toy deserves the flagship polish?

Judged on: first-10-seconds, visual ceiling, depth/replay loop, reliability risk.

## 1. Resonant Ink — RECOMMENDED FLAGSHIP

- **First 10s:** best in the set. Tap → tone + ripple + bloom is instantly legible
  and gorgeous; the hero seed guarantees a beautiful first paint.
- **Visual ceiling:** highest. Refraction + pigment + rings + grain composes like
  liquid light; every session looks different yet coherent.
- **Depth:** tone × pigment × placement × mic gives a real discovery loop; freeze/
  continue turns accidents into studies.
- **Risk:** low. Bounded impulses/particles, capped uploads, no fragile sim.
- **Polish path:** preset studies, performance replay, finer pigment chemistry.

## 2. Harmonic Garden — runner-up

- Hypnotic and the most "instrument-like" (drag → whole field reorganises).
- Slightly weaker first interaction (needs 2 sources before the magic shows) and
  the shader is the most GPU-hungry. Polish path: guided duet onboarding,
  animated transitions recorder.

## 3. Wavelength Weaver — most reliable, least surprising

- Deterministic, cheapest to run, SVG export is a genuine artifact story.
- But the interaction ceiling is ratio-picking; overture + replay help, yet it
  remains a beautiful plotter rather than a discovery toy.

## 4. Shadow Moiré — most tactile, narrowest range

- Sliding/rotating into rosettes is delightful and the print filmstrip is a lovely
  artifact loop. Range is bounded by 6 patterns × 3 merges; a third plate + blend
  lab would extend it.

## 5. Sonar Fossil — deepest sim, heaviest per-frame CPU

- Persistent terrain is uniquely satisfying, but the field update is the most
  expensive loop and the visual payoff needs 3–4 pulses to arrive.

**Verdict:** polish **Resonant Ink** for the submission — strongest first
interaction, highest visual ceiling, lowest risk — with Harmonic Garden's field
rendering as the backup direction.
